SALE 11 - FREE STARTER WEBSITE

This is a free static prototype for your Sale 11 reselling store.

Included:
- Product listing
- Search
- Cart
- Customer delivery address
- Local order creation
- Mobile-friendly design

Important:
- This version does NOT collect real payments.
- Do not claim that a customer has paid until a real payment gateway is connected.
- Meesho is not automatically connected. You must use an officially permitted reselling/fulfilment process.
- Replace the sample product images and products with products you are allowed to resell.

To publish:
1. Upload index.html, style.css and app.js to a static hosting service that supports your chosen deployment.
2. For a real custom domain and live payments, additional setup/fees may apply.
